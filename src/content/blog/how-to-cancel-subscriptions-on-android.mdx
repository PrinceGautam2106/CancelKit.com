Cancel a Google Play subscription from **Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Select the subscription, choose **Cancel subscription**, and finish the confirmation prompts. Uninstalling the app does not cancel billing.

## Cancel on your Android phone or tablet

1. Open the Google Play Store app.
2. Tap your profile icon. Check that you are using the account that paid for the subscription.
3. Choose **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Select the subscription you want to stop.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.
6. Return to the subscription details and check the cancellation status and access end date. Save the confirmation for your records.

Menu wording can vary. Use [Google’s current cancellation instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) if your screen differs.

## Cancel from a computer or mobile browser

1. Open [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
2. Sign in with the purchasing Google account.
3. Find the subscription and choose **Manage**.
4. Choose **Cancel subscription**, select a reason if asked, and confirm.
5. Check the displayed end date. If cancellation is not confirmed, return to the account or contact support.

## Subscription missing? Find who bills you

Switch between your Google accounts and check each subscription list. Search your purchase receipt to identify the account and billing provider. A charge descriptor alone is not proof of a recurring subscription.

Subscriptions bought directly from a provider need to be cancelled through that provider. Start with our [provider cancellation guides](/cancel/). On Samsung phones, Google Play purchases still use Google Play; Galaxy Store purchases use Samsung’s own account and support flow. Do not assume all Android purchases appear in Play.

## What happens after cancellation?

For most subscriptions, access continues through the period already paid for. Check the specific terms: payment plans and instalment commitments can differ. Cancellation generally stops future renewal; it does not automatically refund earlier charges. Cancel a trial before its displayed renewal deadline and check the confirmation.

## Refunds and unexpected charges

Use [Google Play’s refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) to check eligibility. Rules vary by product, timing and location. A refund request and a cancellation are separate actions. If the charge is not yours, use the support process for unrecognized purchases rather than selecting an inaccurate refund reason.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does deleting an Android app cancel its subscription?

No. Cancel the subscription with the billing provider even if you have uninstalled the app.

### Why is my subscription missing from Google Play?

It may belong to another Google account or be billed directly by the provider. Check other accounts and the original purchase receipt.

### Does cancelling automatically give me a refund?

No. Cancellation and refunds are separate. Check Google Play refund eligibility or contact the provider that billed you.

### Can I cancel without my Android phone?

Yes. Sign in to Google Play subscriptions in a web browser with the purchasing account and follow the cancellation prompts.
