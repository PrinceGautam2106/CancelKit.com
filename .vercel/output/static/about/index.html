Our Mission

We built SubZaps because we got sick of being billed for stuff we forgot

The average American spends $273 every month on subscriptions, and over 42% of people continue paying for subscriptions they no longer use. We are leveling the playing field.

The Problem with Subscriptions

Dark patterns rule the modern internet. Signing up for a recurring free trial takes exactly 3 seconds and one tap with Apple Pay or Google Pay. But cancelling that same subscription requires navigating 8 hidden menus, calling a retention hotline during business hours, or threatening a credit card chargeback.

Companies count on you forgetting. In finance, it's called "subscription leakage" — and it costs consumers billions of dollars each year.
