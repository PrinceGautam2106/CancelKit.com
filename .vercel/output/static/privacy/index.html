Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At CancelKits ("CancelKits", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible from cancelkits.com, safeguarding your personal data and maintaining your trust is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you use our web application, tools, and subscription management services.

We strictly comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Google API & Gmail Data Usage (Strict Limited Use Disclosure)

Crucial Gmail Read-Only Access Disclosure:

CancelKits requests access exclusively to the read-only scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. We scan strictly for transactional receipts and subscription billing notices. We literally cannot send, compose, delete, or modify any emails in your account.

In accordance with Google's requirements, our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Specific Scanning Purpose: Our algorithms scan your inbox headers and email message bodies exclusively to extract subscription vendor names, billing amounts, currency, and renewal dates.
  • No Human Reading: No human employees, contractors, or agents at CancelKits read your email messages unless you have given explicit consent for troubleshooting or it is required for security investigations.
  • No Advertising or Resale: We do not transfer, license, sell, or disclose your email data or subscription data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or information aggregators.
  • No AI Model Training: Your email data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

3. What Information We Collect and Store

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide subscription detection and management:

  • Account Identifiers: Your email address and authenticated user ID provided during signup or OAuth authentication.
  • Detected Subscription Data: Vendor/merchant name (e.g., Netflix, Spotify), recurring charge amount, billing interval (monthly, annual), next renewal date, and cancellation status.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payment details are processed directly and securely by Stripe. We never store raw credit or debit card numbers on our servers. We receive only tokenized customer identifiers and subscription statuses.
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser type, device information, and diagnostic server logs retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, and performance monitoring.

5. Data Retention & One-Click Deletion Process

You retain absolute ownership of your personal data. We store your subscription details only for as long as your account remains active.

Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17 & CCPA): You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through your Account Settings (Danger Zone → "Delete all my data"), or by emailing privacy@cancelkits.com. Upon confirmation:

  • All detected subscription records are permanently wiped from our database.
  • OAuth tokens granting inbox access are immediately revoked.
  • Your user record is purged within 30 days from all backup systems.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential authentication cookies (such as sb-access-token), security tokens, and privacy-preserving analytics cookies to maintain your login session and improve application performance. For complete disclosures, please consult our dedicated Cookie Policy.

7. California Consumer Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right under the CCPA to request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. CancelKits does not sell or share your personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration.
