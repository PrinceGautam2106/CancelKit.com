Frequently Asked Questions

Does cancelling LastPass immediately stop charges?

Usually no. After you cancel LastPass, you typically keep access until the end of the current billing period, then renewals stop. Always check your confirmation email for the exact end date.

How long does it take to cancel LastPass?

Most people finish in about 3 minutes when canceling online. Harder plans can take longer if retention calls or in-person visits are required.

Will LastPass offer a discount if I try to cancel?

Yes — LastPass often shows a retention discount or pause offer before canceling completes. You can accept it or decline and finish canceling.
