If you see an unexpected charge on your bank statement labeled **"GOOGLE *[App Name]"** or **"GOOGLE PLAY"**, you are caught in an active recurring Android subscription. Millions of Android users download utility apps, mobile games, VPNs, or streaming trials, delete the app icon from their home screen, and assume the billing automatically stops.

**Uninstalling an app from your Android device does NOT cancel your subscription.** Google will continue billing your saved credit card, PayPal, or carrier billing method month after month until you formally cancel it.

Here is the exact, step-by-step walkthrough to find, manage, and cancel every Google Play and Android subscription in under 60 seconds.

---

## Quick Steps: Cancel Any Android Subscription via Google Play

If you have your Android phone or tablet in hand, here is the fastest way:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app on your phone.
2. Tap your **profile icon** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**.
4. Tap **Subscriptions**.
5. Tap the active subscription you want to cancel.
6. Tap **Cancel subscription** at the bottom of the screen.
7. Select a cancellation reason (or choose *"Decline to answer"*) and tap **Continue**.
8. Tap **Cancel subscription** one last time to confirm.

Google will display a confirmation message with the date your access ends. **You will not be billed again**, but you keep full access to the premium features until the end of your prepaid billing period.

## How to Cancel Google Play Subscriptions from a Web Browser (PC or Mac)

If your Android phone is lost, broken, or out of reach, you can cancel any Google subscription from any computer or mobile browser:

1. Open your web browser and go to [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
2. Sign in with the exact Google account linked to your Android device.
3. Find the subscription under **Active Subscriptions**.
4. Click **Manage** next to the service.
5. Click **Cancel subscription**.
6. Follow the prompt to confirm.

## How to Cancel Subscriptions on Samsung Galaxy Devices

If you own a Samsung Galaxy phone or tablet, some subscriptions (such as Samsung Cloud, font packs, themes, or Galaxy-exclusive apps) are billed through the **Samsung Galaxy Store**, not Google Play.

To check and cancel Samsung Galaxy Store subscriptions:

1. Open the **Galaxy Store** app.
2. Tap the **Menu** tab (three horizontal lines) in the bottom right corner.
3. Tap **Subscriptions** under the My Galaxy section.
4. Select the active service and tap **Cancel subscription**.
5. Tap **Confirm** to lock in your cancellation.

## Why Doesn't the Subscription Appear in Google Play?

If you opened **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions** and the list is blank (or the specific service is missing), one of three things happened:

### 1. You Subscribed on the Web Directly
Major platforms like **Netflix, Spotify, Hulu, YouTube TV, and Adobe** deliberately bypass Google Play's 15–30% billing fee. If you signed up through a browser on their website, Google has zero record of the subscription.
- You must cancel directly in your account settings on the provider's website.
- Browse our collection of 50+ free, verified [Cancel Guides](/cancel/) for one-click cancellation shortcuts and instructions.

### 2. You Have Multiple Google Accounts
Most Android users have more than one Gmail account logged into their phone (e.g., personal, work, school). Google Play attaches the subscription exclusively to the account that was active in the Play Store at the time of purchase.
- In the Play Store, tap your avatar in the upper right corner.
- Switch to your other Google account and check the Subscriptions tab again.

### 3. The Charge Came from PayPal or a Direct Card Draft
Some apps use third-party payment processors like Stripe or PayPal directly inside the app. Check your bank statement: if the descriptor says `"PAYPAL *VENDOR"` or the company's direct legal name rather than `"GOOGLE"`, you must cancel through the vendor's site or your PayPal pre-approved payments dashboard.

## How to Get a Refund for an Accidental Android Charge

If you were billed after forgetting to cancel a free trial within the last 48 hours, Google provides a self-service refund tool:

1. Go to Google's [Play Store Refund Portal](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/9813244).
2. Confirm the Google account that made the purchase.
3. Select the accidental subscription charge from your recent transactions.
4. Choose the reason (e.g., *"I made this purchase by accident"* or *"I didn't intend to renew"*).
5. Submit the request.

Decisions are usually returned within 15 minutes to 4 business days. For more tips on recovering lost subscription fees, read our comprehensive guide on [how to get a refund for an accidental subscription](/blog/how-to-get-refund-for-subscription/).
