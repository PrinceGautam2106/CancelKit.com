If you see an unexpected charge on your credit card labeled **"APPLE.COM/BILL"**, you are caught in the iOS subscription loop. Millions of iPhone users sign up for free 3-day app trials, fitness apps, streaming platforms, or cloud storage, delete the app, and assume the billing stops. 

**Deleting an app from your home screen does NOT cancel your subscription.**

Here is the exact, step-by-step walkthrough to find, manage, and cancel every Apple and App Store subscription on your iPhone in under 60 seconds.

---

## Quick Steps: Cancel Any iPhone Subscription

If you just need the fast path, here it is:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap **Your Name** (Apple ID banner) at the very top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select the active subscription you want to end.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription** (or **Cancel Free Trial**).
6. Tap **Confirm**.

Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period, but **Apple will never bill your card again**.

## What to Do If You Don't See the Subscription Listed

If you opened **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions** and the service is missing, one of three things happened:

### 1. You Subscribed on the Web, Not Through Apple
Services like **Netflix, Spotify, Hulu, Adobe, and Planet Fitness** do not let users subscribe via Apple because of the 30% App Store fee. 
- You must cancel directly on the company's website.
- Check our dedicated [Cancel Guides](/cancel) for step-by-step instructions for over 50+ major services.

### 2. You Have Multiple Apple IDs
Check whether you have a secondary Apple ID or Family Sharing account. Subscriptions belong to the specific Apple ID logged in when the purchase was approved.

### 3. The Subscription Was Processed via Google Play or Amazon
If you recently migrated from Android or registered on an Amazon Fire tablet, check Google Play Store or Amazon's Digital Services hub.

## How to Cancel Apple Subscriptions on a Mac

If your iPhone is out of reach, you can cancel directly from macOS:

1. Open the **App Store** app on your Mac.
2. Click your **name** or **Sign In** button at the bottom left.
3. Click **Account Settings** at the top right.
4. Scroll down to the **Manage** section and click **Manage** next to Subscriptions.
5. Click **Edit** next to the subscription and select **Cancel Subscription**.

## How to Get a Refund from Apple for an Accidental Charge

Did a free trial renew into an expensive annual plan today? Apple provides a formal refund portal:

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
2. Sign in with your Apple ID and password.
3. Under *"What can we help you with?"*, select **"Request a refund"**.
4. In the second dropdown, choose your reason: *"I didn't mean to renew a subscription"* or *"My purchase didn't work as expected"*.
5. Select the charge and hit **Submit**.

Apple typically reviews and approves digital subscription refund requests within **48 hours**. Funds return to your original payment method.
