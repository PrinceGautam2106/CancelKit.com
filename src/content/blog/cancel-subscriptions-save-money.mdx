Want a raise without asking your boss? **Cancel subscriptions and save money** on costs you already proved you can live without.

This is not minimalism theater. It is a cash-flow cleanup.

## Step 1: Know the number you are fighting

Add every recurring charge. Include annual plans as monthly equivalents.

Write two totals:

- **Keep:** used in the last 14 days  
- **Cut:** everything else  

The Cut total is your raise.

## Step 2: Rank cancels by dollars per minute

Cancel in this order:

1. **High price, low use** (Adobe you opened twice, premium gym)  
2. **Trials that converted**  
3. **Duplicates** (two music apps, overlapping streaming)  
4. **Nice-to-haves under $10** that still add up  

A $60 unused tool beats fiddling with a $3 app first.

## Step 3: Use the right cancel door

Wrong door = failed cancel.

- App Store / Play billing must be cancelled in-store settings  
- Carrier-bundled streaming may need the carrier  
- Gym contracts may need in-person or certified mail  

Follow a verified path: browse [CancelKit cancel guides](/cancel) for Netflix, Spotify, Adobe, gyms, VPNs, and more.

## Step 4: Decline the retention maze

Expect discounts, “pause instead,” and guilt screens. If your reason is “I do not use this,” decline and finish.

Save the confirmation. Check one more statement cycle.

## Step 5: Redirect the savings (or it disappears)

When $80/month frees up:

- Auto-transfer $80 to savings the day after payday  
- Or prepay a high-interest debt  

Otherwise lifestyle creep eats the win by Thursday.

## Realistic savings examples

| Cut list | Monthly | Yearly |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Unused streaming + music | $28 | $336 |
| Creative Cloud you replaced | $60 | $720 |
| Trial trio that converted | $35 | $420 |
| **Total** | **$123** | **$1,476** |

That is a substantial vacation fund from decisions you can finish this week.

## Keep a lean stack on purpose

Rules that work:

- One primary video service at a time (rotate seasonally)  
- One cloud storage paid plan  
- Software seats only while a project is active  
- Family plans only if seats are filled
