Research & Methodology

How We Score Subscription Cancel Difficulty

Our consumer research team continuously tests cancellation processes across the 100 most popular consumer subscription services in North America and Europe.

1–4

Frictionless / Easy

Can be cancelled online directly in user settings in under 3 minutes. Zero mandatory surveys or customer support chats required.

5–7

Moderate Resistance

Requires dodging multiple retention discount popups, multi-step exit questionnaires, or live chat wait queues with retention bots.

8–10

Deliberate Hostility

Requires physical in-person visits, certified mail, early termination penalty fees, or mandatory phone calls to aggressive sales agents.
