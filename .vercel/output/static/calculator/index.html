Consumer Benchmark Study

How much does the average American spend on subscriptions?

$219/month

The average American spends $219/month ($2,628/year) on recurring subscriptions, according to benchmark consumer audits by C+R Research and West Monroe.

The 2.5x Blindspot: When consumers are asked to guess their monthly subscription expenditure without reviewing statements, the average estimate is only $86/month. Nearly 42% of consumers admit they continue paying for services they have stopped using entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions About Subscription Costs

How much does the average American spend on subscriptions?

According to comprehensive research by C+R Research and West Monroe, the average American spends approximately $219 per month on subscriptions — totaling over $2,628 per year. Strikingly, when asked to estimate without checking their statements, consumers guessed an average of just $86 per month. This means most people underestimate their recurring subscription costs by 2.5x.

What subscriptions do I have and how can I find them?

To identify all active subscriptions: (1) Check iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; (2) Open Google Play Store → Payments & Subscriptions; (3) Search your email receipts for keywords like "receipt", "subscription", "membership", or "billing period"; (4) Review the past 3 months of bank and credit card statements for recurring monthly merchant names; or (5) Use SubZaps to securely scan your inbox receipts automatically in 30 seconds.

How can I reduce my monthly subscription costs?

The most effective strategies include: (1) Rotate video streaming services month-to-month rather than paying for 4–5 simultaneously; (2) Cancel auto-renewals on free trials immediately after signing up; (3) Audit subscriptions quarterly to prune unused gym memberships or SaaS tools; (4) Request retention discount offers before finalizing cancellations; and (5) Use SubZaps to handle cancellations for difficult companies that demand phone calls or certified mail.

Why does the 5-year subscription cost matter?

Small recurring fees have huge compound impacts on personal savings. For instance, spending $180/month on subscriptions adds up to $10,800 in cash over 5 years. If that same $180/month were invested in a broad-market index fund with average 8% returns, it would grow to nearly $13,200. Seeing the 5-year cost exposes the true financial tradeoff of forgotten memberships.
